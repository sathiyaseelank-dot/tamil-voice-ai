## 1. Executive Summary & Forensic Grounding

### 1.1 Forensic Review of Human Listening vs. Objective Metrics
A critical qualitative discrepancy emerged during the native listening assessment of the Milestone A readiness artifacts, prompting an immediate reassessment of our architectural assumptions:

| Audio Stream | Time-Aligned SNR | SI-SDR | Human Listening Assessment | Diagnostic Verdict |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **GT Codec Reference** | $0.00\text{ dB}$ | $0.00\text{ dB}$ | **Clearly listenable Tamil speech** | Clean ground-truth ceiling |
| **Random Init (4-CB)** | $-2.54\text{ dB}$ | $-42.08\text{ dB}$ | **Robotic, but identifiable Tamil** | Coarse phonetics + acoustic harmonics intact |
| **MMS Init (4-CB)** | $-2.58\text{ dB}$ | $-59.97\text{ dB}$ | **Robotic, but identifiable Tamil** | Coarse phonetics + acoustic harmonics intact |

### 1.2 Forensic Resolution of the SI-SDR Illusion & RVQ Ablation Bug
In the initial investigation, zeroing residual codebooks produced a **$+8.01\text{ dB}$ gain in SI-SDR** ($-26.71\text{ dB}$ vs. $-34.72\text{ dB}$). A deep-dive audit identified two compounding root causes:
1. **SI-SDR High-Frequency Penalty:** Scale-Invariant SDR evaluates time-domain macroscopic energy alignment. Removing high-frequency residual codebooks creates an overly smooth waveform that mathematically aligns with low-frequency energy but strips the vocal tract formants, plosives, and sibilants required for Tamil syllable recognition.
2. **The Encodec Token ID 0 Pseudo-Ablation Bug:** In previous experimental code, CB0-only audio was generated by taking a 4-codebook tensor and substituting token ID 0 for codebooks 1..3 (`pred_tokens[:, 1:, :] = 0`). In Encodec's `ResidualVectorQuantization`, **token ID 0 is not a zero vector**; it is an active codebook vector with non-zero norm ($\approx 32.88$). This pseudo-ablation actively injected arbitrary distortion vectors. True CB0-only decoding requires genuinely truncating the token tensor to $(B, 1, T)$ so that Encodec sums only layer 0 vectors.
3. **Statistical Convergence & WSP Non-Replication:** In expanded 32-utterance validation benchmarking, WSP CB0 accuracy converged to $30.52\%$, falling below random ($32.90\%$) and MMS ($33.80\%$). The two-stage strategy cannot be assumed validated a priori; it must be empirically audited.

## 3. Experiment A: CB0-Only Learning Architecture & Protocol

### 3.1 Experimental Objective
Test whether a dedicated Transformer predictor trained solely to predict CB0 on the streaming Tamil dataset can achieve high phonetic accuracy ($> 60\%$) and whether genuine CB0-only decoded audio ($n_q = 1$) can produce intelligible Tamil speech.

### 3.2 Specifications
- **Module:** [`s2s_v2/exp_a_cb0_learning.py`](file:///content/tamil-voice-ai/s2s_v2/exp_a_cb0_learning.py)
- **Model:** `S2STokenPredictor` ($d=1024, 6\text{ layers}, 16\text{ heads}, d_{ff}=4096$).
- **Loss Formulation:** $\lambda_0 = 1.0, \; \lambda_1 = 0.0, \; \lambda_2 = 0.0, \; \lambda_3 = 0.0$.
- **Codec State:** Encodec 24 kHz frozen ($0$ trainable parameters).
- **Checkpoints:** Evaluated at steps 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000.
- **Audio Output:** Genuine CB0-only decoding ($n_q = 1$) vs. 4-codebook ground-truth reference.
- **Metric Requirement:** SI-SDR is disqualified as a primary pass/fail metric. Human intelligibility and offline ASR CER are primary.

## 4. Experiment B: Residual Codebook Learning & Conditioning Decoupling

To prevent confounding teacher forcing with inference performance, Experiment B is strictly decoupled into two separate evaluation protocols:

### 4.1 Condition B1: Teacher-Forced Ceiling (Diagnostic)
- **Conditioning:** Ground-truth CB0 ($Y_0^*$) fed into `DepthAudioHead`.
- **Decoding:** Ground-truth CB0 ($Y_0^*$) combined with predicted residuals ($\hat{Y}_{1..3}$).
- **Comparison:** Compared against Ground-Truth CB0-only decoding ($[Y_0^*]$).
- **Purpose:** Establishes the theoretical upper bound of residual codebook acoustic enhancement when the phonetic backbone is $100\%$ perfect.

### 4.2 Condition B2: True Inference Condition
- **Conditioning:** Predicted CB0 ($\hat{Y}_0$) fed into `DepthAudioHead` (zero ground-truth tokens).
- **Decoding:** Predicted CB0 ($\hat{Y}_0$) combined with predicted residuals ($\hat{Y}_{1..3}$).
- **Comparison:** Compared against Predicted CB0-only decoding ($[\hat{Y}_0]$).
- **Purpose:** Measures actual real-world performance under realistic inference error propagation.

### 4.3 Diagnostic B3: Cross-Condition Sensitivity Analysis
- Evaluates the impact of ground-truth conditioning with predicted decoding to measure decoding sensitivity vs. conditioning sensitivity.
- **Module:** [`s2s_v2/exp_b_residual_learning.py`](file:///content/tamil-voice-ai/s2s_v2/exp_b_residual_learning.py)

## 6. Human Listening Evaluation & Agreement Protocol

### 6.1 Evaluation Methodology
- **Module:** [`s2s_v2/human_listening_evaluator.py`](file:///content/tamil-voice-ai/s2s_v2/human_listening_evaluator.py)
- **Blinding & Presentation:** Audio clips normalized to $-20\text{ dBFS}$ RMS, assigned randomized SHA-256 alphanumeric hashes, and presented in shuffled order.
- **Dual Likert Assessment:**
  1. **Intelligibility (1–5):** (1: Unintelligible noise, 2: Isolated phonemes, 3: Partial words, 4: Clear with accent, 5: Flawless native Tamil).
  2. **Naturalness & Cleanliness (1–5):** (1: Harsh phase distortion, 2: Robotic & muffled, 3: Moderate buzz, 4: Natural with minor artifacts, 5: Clean studio speech).
- **Listener Transcript Entry:** Native Tamil listeners transcribe what they heard; the module computes CER and WER against reference text.
- **Inter-Listener Agreement Reporting:** Computes Fleiss' Kappa ($\kappa$), Krippendorff's Alpha ($\alpha$), and Spearman rank correlation across rater evaluations.
